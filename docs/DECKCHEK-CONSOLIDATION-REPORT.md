## Passive support A/B observations and comparisons

The versioned method `support-lf-welch-ms-v1` analyzes complete, half-overlapping periodic-Hann windows from the first 30 seconds, with per-segment mean removal. It integrates one-sided PSD over 20–200 Hz using each bin cell's overlap in Hz. L/R powers are independent; pair power is their arithmetic mean. The directional result is the difference of finite logarithmic levels, **B minus A**; negative means lower recorded digital band power in B. Actual analyzed coverage and omitted frames are retained, while quality checks inspect every returned sample, including the unanalyzed suffix.
